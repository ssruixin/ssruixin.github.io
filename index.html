关于我

Ruixin Sun

我叫 Ruixin Sun，来自中国江苏徐州。我是一名视觉艺术创作者与策展人，目前就读于香港城市大学创意媒体专业（策展方向），毕业于加拿大布鲁克大学视觉艺术（艺术工作室）专业。

我的创作横跨绘画、摄影、数字艺术与策展，关注情感表达、身体经验与当代视觉文化。我相信艺术不仅是观看的对象，更是人与人、人与空间建立连接的媒介。

2024 年，我在江苏省首届胸痛中心艺术展“心跳节律：艺术的召唤”中获得一等奖，也曾在徐州美术馆、徐州广电及江苏易点通传媒参与展览执行与内容创作工作。

My name is Ruixin Sun, from Xuzhou, Jiangsu Province, China. I am a visual artist and curator currently studying Creative Media (Curatorial Branch) at City University of Hong Kong, and I hold a BA in Visual Arts (Art Studio) from Brock University, Canada.

My practice spans painting, photography, digital art, and curatorial work, focusing on emotional expression, bodily experience, and contemporary visual culture. I believe art is not only something to be seen, but also a medium that connects people and spaces.

In 2024, I received the First Prize at the Jiangsu Province Chest Pain Center Art Exhibition “Rhythm of the Heart: The Call of Art.” I have also worked on exhibition execution and content creation at Xuzhou Art Museum, Xuzhou Radio and Television Media Group, and Jiangsu Yi Dian Tong Media Planning Co.
